# Maple-Soy Glazed Salmon with Roasted Garlic

<img src="/_pics/salmon_maple.jpg" alt="Maple-Soy Glazed Salmon" align="left" width="25%" />

A delicious salmon dish made with what is probably my favorite ingredient,
roasted garlic. See [Roasted Garlic](/quickies/roasted_garlic.md) for how
to make the roasted garlic.

<br clear="left"/>

## Ingredients:

### For the Glaze:

* 1/4 cup Real Maple Syrup
* 2 tablespoons Soy Sauce
* 1 tablespoon Kewpie Mayo
* 3-4 cloves Roasted Garlic, mashed into a smooth paste
* 1 teaspoon Rice Vinegar (or Apple Cider Vinegar)
* Freshly ground Black Pepper, to taste

### For the Salmon:

* 4 center-cut salmon fillets (about 6 oz each)
* Salt and Black Pepper
* 1 tablespoon Neutral Oil (vegetable, canola, or avocado oil) for searing
* Optional garnish: Sesame seeds, sliced green onions, pickled ginger, or fresh herbs

## Instructions:

1. **Make the Glaze:**<br/>
    In a small bowl, whisk together the maple syrup, soy sauce, Kewpie mayo, mashed roasted garlic, rice vinegar, and black pepper until smooth and well combined. Set aside.

2. **Prep the Salmon:**<br/>
    Pat the salmon fillets dry with paper towels. Season both sides lightly with salt and black pepper.

3. **Sear the Salmon**:<br/>
    Heat the oil in a large oven-safe skillet (cast iron works great) over medium-high heat. Once the oil is shimmering, place the salmon fillets skin-side up in the pan. Sear for 3-4 minutes until a golden crust forms on the bottom.

4. **Flip and Glaze:**<br/>
    Carefully flip the fillets so they're skin-side down. Brush a generous amount of the glaze over the top of each fillet, reserving some for later.

5. **Finish in the Oven:**<br/>
    Transfer the skillet to a preheated 400°F (200°C) oven. Roast for 6-8 minutes, depending on thickness, until the salmon is cooked to your desired doneness (internal temp of 125-130°F for medium-rare, 135-140°F for medium).

6. **Final Glaze:**<br/>
    Remove from the oven and brush the fillets with another layer of the glaze while they're still hot. Let rest for 2 minutes.

7. **Serve:**<br/>
    Plate the salmon and drizzle any remaining glaze over the top. Garnish with sesame seeds, sliced green onions, pickled ginger, or fresh herbs if desired.

Pro Tip: If you don't have roasted garlic on hand, you can quickly roast a few cloves by wrapping them in foil with a drizzle of olive oil and baking at 400°F for about 20-25 minutes until soft and golden.
