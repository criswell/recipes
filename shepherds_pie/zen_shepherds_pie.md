# The "Zen" Shepherd's Pie (Wasabi & Ginger)

A Japanese-inspired twist with a sharp, aromatic profile.

Spicy mashed potatoes, replace the peas with edamame, replace the carrots with
shiitake mushrooms. Cook the meat with ginger, garlic, and onion.

## The Base

* **Protein**: 1 lb ground lamb or beef.
* **Aromatics**: 2 tbsp freshly grated ginger, 3 cloves minced garlic, 1 small onion (diced).
* **Vegetables**: 1 cup fresh shiitake mushrooms (sliced), 1 cup frozen edamame (shelled).
* **Sauce**: 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp mirin, 1 cup beef or vegetable broth thickened with 1 tbsp cornstarch.
* **Method**: Brown the meat with ginger, garlic, and onion. Add mushrooms and edamame. Stir in the sauce ingredients and simmer until thickened.

## The Mash

* **Ingredients**: 2 lbs Yukon Gold potatoes, 1-2 tbsp wasabi paste (adjust to your heat preference), 1/4 cup coconut milk or butter, splash of rice vinegar.
* **Method**: Boil and mash potatoes. Fold in wasabi, coconut milk, and vinegar until smooth and pale green.

## The Assembly

* Spread the ginger-meat base in a baking dish. Top with the wasabi mash. Sprinkle with furikake or toasted sesame seeds. Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 20-25 minutes until the edges are bubbling.

Pro Tip: If you want a crispier top, you can turn on the broiler for the last 2-3 minutes of cooking- just keep a close eye on it so it doesn't burn!
