# FAQ and Q&A

## Have you tried all these recipes?

Yes!

Every recipe here is something I've made and fed my family. The criteria for
sharing is basically 2/3rd of my family had to have enjoyed it.

## Why do some recpies have pictures and others don't?

Sometimes the dish isn't terribly photogenic.

Sometimes we enjoy the food so much we just eat it before I think to take a
picture.

Who knows the exact reason why?

## Did you really make all these recipes?

Short answer: Yes.

Long answer: Kinda.

I tend to have these weird ideas like "What if we take a Shepherd's Pie but add
a Japanese twist?"

I take these ideas, and start researching. I find flavors and alternatives to
the traditional ingredients that fit the theme I've come up with. Then, once I
have a solid foundation, I sit with an LLM (because I'm a software engineer, and
when the only tool you have is a hammer, everything looks like a nail) and start
to formulate a workable recipe from my weird ideas.

So, yes, I did come up with the idea for the dish as well as the ingredients
all on my own, but the polishing of that idea into an actual recipe is a
co-operative endeavour with an LLM.

This means there easily could be recipes from the internet that the LLM is
pulling from. So I can't say with certainty that 100% of the recipe is my
creation. But they mostly are, and that's got to count for something.

Additionally, there's a few recipes (Tamagoyaki is a great example) where I
took an existing recipe of a pretty common food and tweaked it.

## Can I contribute to this repo my own recipes?

I don't plan on taking PRs for this repo to add new recipes. If you have your
own recipes, make your own repo and tell me! I'll gladly link to it.

These recipes are supposed to be "my recipes", e.g. things I tried to create
myself. I wouldn't want to include someone elses and then try to pass it off
as my own.

## So no PRs?

I will accept PRs that correct things. Like, if you find something spelled
wrong, or if I typo'd an ingredient and the amount to use is wrong. All that
stuff I'd like fixed, so if you find one, feel free to open a PR to fix it!
