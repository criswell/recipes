# Roasted Garlic

Simple ingredient to make that can really elevate just about any dish.

## Ingredients

* Garlic cloves (as many as you need)
* Cooking Oil

Your oil can really be anything. The most common to use is traditional olive oil.
However, if you have any infused oil, you can use that as well. Just consider
the flavor the oil will bring to the garlic, but don't worry about it clashing
too much because sometimes crazy combos can be good (I like mandarin infused
olive oil a lot with garlic).

## Instructions

1. Cut the top off of the garlic clove. Leave the garlic husk on. What you're
wanting here is a cup that contains the garlic.

2. Pour your oil into the opening you cut at the top of the clove. Be generous
with the oil, don't be afraid to spill. You want it filled as much as you can
with your oil of choice.

3. Wrap your garlic bulb filled with oil tightly in aluminum foil.

4. Heat your oven to 400°F (200°C). A toaster oven is fine, you'll only be placing
your garlic clove(s) in it.

5. Place the foil wrapped garlic in the oven. Cook for 20-25 minutes until soft
and golden brown.
