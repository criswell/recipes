# Short-rib Grilled Cheese, rainbow carrot fries, and three dipping sauces.

A buttery, short-rib grilled cheese sandwich. Pair it with roasted rainbow
carrot fries. Finish it all off with three decadent dipping sauces. Traditional?
Not quite. Tasty? Absolutely.

## Ingredients

### Short Ribs

* 3-4 lbs bone‑in beef short ribs
* 1 large onion, sliced
* 3 cloves garlic, smashed
* 2 carrots, roughly chopped
* 2 cups beef broth
* 1 cup red wine (optional; sub broth if needed)
* 2 tbsp tomato paste
* 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
* 2 tsp salt
* 1 tsp black pepper
* 2 tbsp oil (for searing)

### Sandwich Components

* Sourdough or similar sturdy bread
* 1-1.5 cups shredded Gruyère
* 1-1.5 cups shredded soft cheddar (young cheddar or mild cheddar)
* 4 tbsp butter (for garlic butter)
* 1 garlic clove, grated or minced
* Optional: crispy onions or arugula inside the sandwich

### Rainbow Carrot Fries

* 1.5-2 lbs rainbow carrots
* 2 tbsp olive oil
* 1 tsp salt
* ½ tsp black pepper

### Dipping Sauces

1. Peppercorn Ranch

  * ½ cup ranch dressing
  * ½-1 tsp coarse cracked black pepper
  * Optional: a splash of lemon juice

2. Smoked Paprika & Honey Butter

  * 3 tbsp butter
  * ½ tsp smoked paprika
  * 1 tsp honey
  * Pinch of salt

3. Whipped Feta with Hot Honey

  * 4 oz feta
  * 2 tbsp Greek yogurt or sour cream
  * 1 tbsp olive oil
  * 1-2 tsp hot honey
  * Optional: lemon zest

---

## Cooking Plan (Total time: ~3 hours, mostly hands-off)

### 1. Braise the Short Ribs (2.5 hours total)

Start with this - everything else happens while they cook.

1. Preheat oven to 325°F (160°C).
2. Heat a large Dutch oven over medium‑high heat with oil.
3. Season ribs with salt & pepper, then sear all sides until deeply browned (8-10 min).
4. Remove ribs; add onions, carrots, garlic. Sauté 3-4 min.
5. Stir in tomato paste for 1 minute.
6. Add broth + wine + Worcestershire. Bring to a simmer.
7. Add ribs back in, cover, and place in oven.
8. Braise for 2.5 hours, turning once halfway.

*They should be fall‑apart tender.*

**Parallel Task While They Cook**: Prep sauces, shred cheeses, prep carrots.

---

### 2. Prepare the Dipping Suaces (20 minutes, anytime during braise)

**Peppercorn Ranch**

Mix ranch + cracked pepper + optional lemon juice. Chill.

**Smoked Paprika Honey Butter**

Melt butter, whisk in paprika, honey, pinch of salt. Keep warm or rewarm before serving.

**Whipped Feta**

Blend feta, yogurt, and olive oil until smooth. Drizzle hot honey on top.

*Set all sauces aside or refrigerate.*

---

### 3. Prep the Carrot Fries (during last 45 min of short rib braise)

1. Increase oven temp to **425°F (220°C)** once ribs come out later.
2. Peel carrots and slice into sticks (½‑inch thick).
3. Toss with olive oil, salt, pepper.
4. Spread on 1-2 sheets - don’t overcrowd.

*(Wait to roast until ribs come out and you increase the oven temp.)*

---

### 4. Finish the Short Ribs (0–15 minutes after braise)

1. Remove ribs from the oven.
2. Pull the meat off the bone with forks; discard excess fat and connective tissue.
3. Optionally reduce the braising liquid on the stovetop for dipping.

*Set shredded short rib aside.*

---

### 5. Roast the Carrot Fries (25–30 minutes)

After removing ribs, raise oven to **425°F (220°C)**.

Roast carrots for **25-30 minutes**, flipping halfway, until slightly caramelized and tender.

**Parallel Task While Carrots Roast:** Assemble and cook sandwiches.

---

### 6. Make the Garlic Butter

Microwave or lightly melt 4 tbsp butter with minced garlic. Brush inside and outside of the bread.

---

### 7. Assemble & Cook the Grilled Cheese (15 minutes)

1. Heat a skillet or griddle on medium or medium‑low.
2. Butter bread generously.
3. Layer Gruyère + cheddar + warm short rib.
4. Cook slow and low for maximum melt (4-6 minutes per side).
5. Press gently with a spatula; cover with a lid briefly if needed.

You want crisp, golden bread and fully melted cheese.

---

### 8. Serve

* Sandwiches cut diagonally (for dipping).
* Carrot fries in a big bowl or board.
* Sauce trio in small ramekins.

Everything should finish around the same time.
