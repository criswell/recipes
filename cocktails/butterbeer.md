## Butterbeer Rum Float

* 1.5 oz dark rum
* 3-4 oz root beer (or cream soda for sweeter vibe)
* splash butterbeer creamer

Build over ice, gently stir. Top with a big swirl of butterbeer whipped cream.

## Butterbeer White Russian

* 1.5 oz spiced rum (or dark rum)
* 1 oz coffee liqueur (like Kahlúa)
* 2 oz milk or half & half
* 112 oz butterbeer creamer

Shake with ice, strain over fresh ice. Top with your butterbeer whipped cream.

## 🔥 Hot Buttered Butterbeer Rum

* 1.5 oz spiced rum
* 4 oz hot milk (or hot water + milk mix)
* 2 oz butterbeer creamer
* pinch cinnamon or nutmeg

Stir in a mug, top with whipped cream.

## Butterbeer Espresso Martini (if you want fancy)

* 1 oz spiced rum
* 1 oz coffee liqueur
* 1 oz espresso
* 1 oz butterbeer creamer

Shake hard with ice, strain into a glass, top with whipped cream
