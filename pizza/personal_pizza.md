# Personal Pizzas for a Group

<img src="/_pics/pizza_personal.jpg" alt="Pizza prep station" align="left" width="25%" />

I'm not much of a fan of pizza. That being said, I really like unusual pizzas.
Anchovies, pineapple, artrichoke, balsamic vinegrette... these all make great
pizza ingredients IMHO.

However, not everyone agrees with me. Especially not my family.

So please everyone by letting each person put their own friggin' toppings on.

You will need enough baking sheets to fit all the pizzas, and an oven large
enough for them. If you need to stagger cooking the pizzas, I'll leave that for
you to figure out.

I also have two paths for the dough. You either make your own, or buy from the
store.

<br clear="left"/>

## Ingredients List

**For the Homemade Dough (Makes 3 Personal Pizzas)**

* 2 ¼ cups all-purpose flour or bread flour (plus extra for dusting)
* 1 cup warm water (about 105°F-115°F)
* 1 packet (2 ¼ tsp) active dry yeast
* 1 tsp sugar (to feed the yeast)
* 1 tsp salt
* 2 tbsp olive oil

**For the Store-Bought Shortcut**

* 1.5 lbs of pre-made pizza dough (usually sold in 1lb or 1.5lb bags in the deli section). Divide this into three equal 8oz balls.

**Topping Station Ideas**

* Sauce: 1 cup pizza sauce or crushed San Marzano tomatoes.
* Cheese: 2 cups shredded mozzarella (or a mix of mozzarella and parmesan).
* Meats: Pepperoni, cooked Italian sausage, ham, or bacon bits.
* Veggies: Sliced bell peppers, red onions, mushrooms, black olives, or fresh basil.

I'd start with these safe (and boring) options. But feel free to adjust or add
anything unique or awesome you'd like!

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Prepare the Dough**

**If making homemade:**

In a large bowl, combine warm water, sugar, and yeast. Let it sit for 5-10 minutes until foamy. Stir in the olive oil, salt, and flour. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 5 minutes until smooth and elastic. Place in a lightly oiled bowl, cover with a damp cloth, and let rise in a warm spot for 1 to 1.5 hours, or until doubled in size. Punch the dough down and divide into three equal balls.

**If using store-bought:**

Take the dough out of the refrigerator at least 30 minutes before you plan to bake. Cold dough is "snappy" and hard to stretch. Divide the dough into three equal portions and let them rest on a floured surface covered with a towel.

2. **Preheat and Prep the Oven**

Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C). High heat is the secret to a crispy crust. Adjust your oven racks so one is in the upper third and one is in the lower third of the oven. Lightly grease two large baking sheets with olive oil or dust them with cornmeal.

3. **Shape the Pizzas**

On a floured surface, stretch or roll each dough ball into a circle roughly 9-10 inches in diameter. Place two pizzas on one large baking sheet (they can be close but shouldn't touch) and the third pizza on the second baking sheet.

4. **The Topping Station**

Let each family member decorate their own pizza. Start with a thin layer of sauce, followed by a layer of cheese, and then the toppings. Remind everyone not to "over-load" the center, as too many heavy toppings can make the crust soggy.

5. **Baking Instructions**

Place the sheet with two pizzas on the top rack and the sheet with one pizza on the bottom rack. Bake for about 10-12 minutes total.

**Crucial Step**: At the 6-minute mark, rotate the pans- move the top pan to the bottom rack and the bottom pan to the top rack. Also, turn the pans 180 degrees to account for any hot spots in the oven.

6. **Serving**

The pizzas are done when the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly and starting to brown in spots. Let them cool for 2-3 minutes before slicing so the cheese sets.
